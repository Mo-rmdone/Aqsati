# PART A — Pitch content

## The one-liner
**Aqsati is the Arabic-first AI collections platform that makes sure Egyptian installment and credit sellers actually get paid — automating reminders, reconciliation, and receivables for both قسط and آجل.**

## The 30-second elevator
> Millions of Egyptian merchants sell on installments and credit, but they track it on paper and WhatsApp — so collections leak and cash flow suffers. Aqsati is a cloud system that automates the whole collections cycle: smart multichannel reminders, payments that reconcile themselves, a live "who owes me and who's late" dashboard, and a self-service portal where the customer pays. We monetize as pure SaaS. Egypt's consumer-credit market is heading from ~$1.37B to ~$4.16B by 2031, and nobody local has built the collections layer underneath it. We're building it first, in Arabic.

## Slide-by-slide deck (12 slides)

### 1 — Title
- **Aqsati / أقساطي** — "Get paid, automatically."
- Sub: AI collections & receivables for Egypt's credit economy. | Founder name · contact · date.

### 2 — The problem
- Egyptian installment sellers & مكاتب تقسيط run on **paper ledgers (كشكول), Excel, and WhatsApp**.
- Consequences: collections leak, late payments slip, disputes over balances, no visibility into expected vs. overdue cash, re-lending to known bad payers.
- **Pain quote to feature:** an office with 800 live contracts spends hours/day calling customers and still loses money to overdue.

### 3 — Why now (timing)
- Egypt BNPL/consumer-credit: **~$1.37B (2025) → ~$4.16B (2031)** — merchants pulled into selling on credit.
- **Mandatory e-invoicing (ETA)** forcing digitization.
- **WhatsApp + smartphone ubiquity**; **InstaPay/IPN** rails maturing → recurring pull becomes possible.
- The back-office software layer is fragmented and shallow — the window is open now.

### 4 — The solution
- Cloud, Arabic-first system covering **قسط (installment) + آجل (deferred B2B)** in one place.
- Core loop: create contract → auto-schedule → **smart reminders** → **auto-reconcile payments** → **collections dashboard** → customer self-service portal.
- Show the product: dashboard + contract wizard (wireframes/screens).

### 5 — Product / the hook
- **The wedge:** WhatsApp/SMS auto-reminders + the "who owes me / who's late" delinquency dashboard = money collected + hours saved, felt in month 1.
- Feature pillars: Contracts & schedules · Collections engine · Reminders (omnichannel) · Auto-reconciliation · Reports/aging · Multi-branch & collector roles · Investor module.

### 6 — Competitive edge (what nobody local has)
- Reframe: competitors are **record-keepers**; Aqsati is an **outcome machine**.
- Mined from global leaders, absent in MENA:
  - **AI Next-Best-Action collections** (à la TrueAccord) — optimal time/channel/message per customer.
  - **Auto-reconciliation** (à la GoCardless) — payments match themselves.
  - **Auto-debit mandates** — chase → auto-pull.
  - **Two-sided ledger + customer portal** (à la Khatabook — its viral engine to 40M+ merchants).
  - Moat: **credit risk scoring** + **portfolio-at-risk analytics** powered by our compounding dataset.

### 7 — Market (TAM / SAM / SOM)
- **TAM:** all Egyptian businesses selling on installment/credit (retail electronics, furniture, appliances, auto/moto dealers, مكاتب تقسيط, B2B wholesale آجل) — hundreds of thousands of SMBs; riding a multi-$B credit market.
- **SAM:** installment offices + credit-selling SMBs with recurring contracts and >1 collector/branch.
- **SOM (3-yr):** first few thousand paying offices in target governorates.
- *(Insert bottom-up estimate: # target merchants × ARPU. Validate merchant counts before the raise.)*

### 8 — Business model
- **Pure SaaS**, tiered by users/branches with generous contract limits (deliberately NOT metering per-contract like Qasetly).
- Tiers (EGP/yr): Free → Basic ~4,990 → **Pro ~9,990 ⭐** → Business ~24,990 → Enterprise custom.
- **Add-ons:** WhatsApp/SMS bundles, ETA e-invoice, online-payment collection, extra users/branches.
- Sticky (data lock-in), high margin (>80% ex-messaging), expanding ARPU.

### 9 — Go-to-market
- **Land:** field + WhatsApp outreach governorate-by-governorate; free migration from their ledger (kills switching cost + undercuts Qasetli's 2,000 EGP setup fee).
- **Expand:** Facebook/TikTok demo videos of the hook; accountant referral commissions; supplier/POS partnerships.
- **Activation metric:** first automated reminder + first payment logged within 48h.

### 10 — Competition
- Direct: **Qasetly** (closest; per-contract pricing + setup fee), **Aksat** (free, mobile, shallow), **Qest** (deep but Saudi/desktop), **SMACC**, **Daftra** (ERP, installments as a bolt-on).
- Real incumbent: **paper + Excel + WhatsApp**.
- BNPL (valU/Sympl/Halan/Shahry) = **partners, not rivals** (they fund; we're the merchant's system of record).
- **Our moat:** collections-first + dual قسط/آجل + investor module + compounding credit-history dataset.

### 11 — Traction / roadmap / metrics
- Design-partner offices (target 5–10) using it on real contracts; show measurable lift in overdue collected within 60 days (the guarantee metric).
- Roadmap: Tier-A MVP (web) → Tier-B differentiation → Tier-C moat (mobile, auto-debit, risk AI).
- KPIs to show: activation ≥60%, trial→paid ≥20%, logo churn <3%, ARR vs. ~300–400-office break-even (~3–4M EGP ARR).

### 12 — The ask
- Raising **[amount]** to: build web + mobile, hire field sales in 2–3 governorates, reach **~300–500 paying offices** in [N] months.
- Use of funds: product/eng %, GTM/sales %, ops/support %.
- Vision: become the **collections & receivables operating system** for MENA's credit economy — and eventually the credit-scoring layer beneath it.

## Investor Q&A prep
- **"Why won't Daftra/Qasetly just add this?"** → They're accounting/record-first; a collections-first, dual قسط/آجل, portal + auto-reconcile product is a different architecture and go-to-market; per-contract pricing is core to Qasetly's model. We also compound a proprietary collections dataset.
- **"Isn't this just BNPL?"** → No balance-sheet risk. We're SaaS for the *seller's own* book; BNPL players are funders and are partners/integration targets.
- **"How do you collect if customers don't have cards?"** → Cash-first workflows + collector app + Fawry/InstaPay references; auto-debit is upside, not a dependency.
- **"Regulatory risk?"** → We don't lend in v1; pure software. We design compliant, humane collections that pre-empt future consumer-finance regulation.
- **"CAC/scaling?"** → Field + WhatsApp + accountant referrals + viral customer portal; free migration lowers switching cost.
- **"What's the moat in 3 years?"** → Data (credit history/risk scoring), multi-branch/investor lock-in, network effects from the two-sided ledger.
